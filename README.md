🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Using SQL

End-to-End SQL Data Analytics Project | SQL Server | Business Intelligence

📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics project developed using SQL Server to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.

The analysis explores sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, orders, payments, returns, reviews, and shipping operations to identify trends, business opportunities, and areas requiring improvement.

The project demonstrates how SQL can be used not only to retrieve data, but also to solve real-world business problems and support data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of transactional data, but raw data alone does not provide actionable information.

This project aims to answer questions such as:

Which products and categories generate the most revenue?
Who are the highest-value customers?
Which products are underperforming?
How are sales changing over time?
What is the average order value?
Which payment methods are most preferred?
Which products experience high return activity?
How do discounts influence sales?
What patterns can be identified from customer reviews?
Where can the business improve its operational performance?

🗂️ Dataset / Database Structure

The project analyzes an e-commerce relational database containing the following major entities:

Table	Purpose
Customers	Customer information
Orders	Order-level transaction information
Order_Items	Products purchased within each order
Products	Product and category information
Payments	Payment transactions and payment status
Returns	Returned orders/products
Reviews	Customer ratings and reviews
Shipments	Shipping and delivery information

These tables are analyzed together using SQL joins and relational data analysis techniques.

🚀 Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical findings, the business can consider the following strategies:

1. Focus on High-Performing Products

Increase inventory availability and marketing efforts for products generating strong revenue and demand.

2. Improve Underperforming Products

Investigate products with low sales and evaluate pricing, visibility, customer demand, and product positioning.

3. Strengthen Customer Retention

Develop targeted loyalty and retention strategies for high-value customers.

4. Optimize Discount Strategies

Use discounts strategically rather than relying on excessive discounting that may reduce profitability.

5. Reduce Product Returns

Investigate products with unusually high return rates and identify possible issues related to quality, description, pricing, or customer expectations.

6. Improve Shipping Operations

Monitor delayed or unsuccessful shipments and improve delivery processes to enhance customer satisfaction.

7. Monitor Customer Feedback

Use reviews and ratings to identify customer pain points and improve products with consistently low ratings.

8. Use Data-Driven Marketing

Use customer purchasing behavior and product preferences to create more targeted marketing campaigns.
